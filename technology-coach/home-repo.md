# Home repo

Dave's private folder of coaching notes. The **root** is the first folder at or above the working directory that holds `clients/`; paths below are relative to it. Its `AGENTS.md` holds the local rules (how to give paths, confidentiality, where the skill source lives); follow them.

## Layout

```
AGENTS.md                          local rules
CLAUDE.md                          the one line @AGENTS.md
clients/<client>/
  client.md                        client-wide facts, Language line, client scale ↔ levels
  structure.md                     optional: units, mandate, route per unit
  report.md                        generated by Report; never edited by hand
  success-cases/YYYY-MM.md         one per Success Case round
  teams/<team>/                    a group coached over time
  individuals/<first-last>/        one person coached 1-on-1
  meetings/<YYYY-MM-DD-topic>/     a one-off session for a mixed audience
  communities/<name>/              a recurring group with a changing audience
    case.md
    sessions/YYYY-MM-DD-<format>.md   game plan on top, retro below
```

Templates for every file are in this skill's `templates/`. Every file is in English, headings and text alike. The **Language** line in `client.md` only sets the language of what the teams see or hear (script lines, slides).

## Filing rules

- **One place, as high as it holds.** Client-wide facts in `client.md`, unit-wide in the unit's section of `structure.md`, the rest in `case.md`. Link instead of copying.
- **Open only what the task is about**: that client, those cases.
- **Folder names** lowercase with dashes; individuals by first and last name, meetings by date and topic.
- **Case header:** `**Type:** · **Client:** · **Unit:**` as in the templates, linking `client.md` and `structure.md#<unit>`. Without a `structure.md`, leave Unit out. An individual adds **Team** (its case, or "<team>, no case"); meetings and communities name the teams in the room. Other cross-links go in the text where they matter.
- **New case:** copy the template for its type to `<type>s/<name>/case.md`, and name the team in its unit's Structure in `structure.md` if it is not there yet.
- **People notes live in cases**: a team's People table, or an individual case when Dave coaches the person 1-on-1. Someone in two teams: full notes where Dave mainly coaches them, a one-line link in the other. A note with no case to live in stays out until a case needs it.
- **Uncertainty shows by placement** (Hypotheses to test, Open questions, "not clear yet") and in plain words where the source matters ("the previous coach thinks…").
- **Moves and renames:** the case folder keeps its name; update `structure.md`, the Unit link in the header, and add a History line ("moved from unit A to unit B").
- **A meeting that recurs** becomes a community case; the meeting case keeps a History line linking to it.
- **Every change to `case.md`** updates *Last updated* and, when it changes the coaching picture, gets a dated History line.

## Prepare

1. **Read**, in order: `client.md` → the case's unit in `structure.md` → `case.md` → the retro of the latest session. For an individual, also their team's `case.md`; for a meeting or community, the cases of the teams in the room.
2. **Run Prepare** (`prepare.md`) with what you read; ask Dave only what is still missing. Every Starting picture item still "not clear yet" goes into the plan's Questions to ask.
3. **Check the plan** against `client.md` (Session rules, Language, budget, tool approvals) and the unit in `structure.md` (mandate, route agreements such as who joins a session). These beat generic advice. Rewrite what conflicts; tell Dave each conflict, and put the ones the plan cannot solve under Open questions in `case.md`.
4. **Save** to `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD-<format>.md` from `templates/session.md`. File the facts Dave gave while preparing (people, stack, processes) by the filing rules. Add a History line to `case.md`: `YYYY-MM-DD · <format> · planned: <goal of the meeting> → sessions/...`.

**Done when** the session file holds the plan, every conflict is rewritten or under Open questions, Dave's new facts are filed, and `case.md` has the History line.

## Retro

1. **Append** the retro (Retro mode in `SKILL.md`) under Retro in the session file, with its **Evidence** block: Seen, Said and Blockers, each filled in or explicitly "none".
2. **Update `case.md`:** Starting picture items now clear (dated; corrections noted), target behaviours added or retired (each with a History line), Level, Route step, Agreements, Open questions. Replace the session's *planned* History line with its outcome.
3. **Meeting or community:** when someone shows the practice in their work, the Seen entry goes into the session's Evidence of the **team** case where that work happens, linking back to this session.
4. **Climb:** a fact that holds for the whole unit goes to `structure.md`, one that holds client-wide to `client.md`.
5. **Skill proposal** (Retro mode step 5) goes into the session file; once Dave agrees, it lands anonymised in the skill source named in `AGENTS.md`.

**Done when** the Evidence block has all three lines, and every new fact sits in the one file where it holds.

## File a paste

A paste block comes from a machine without a home repo (shape: `SKILL.md`, Saving the work). That machine had no `client.md`, `structure.md` or `case.md`, so filing is also a check.

1. **Find the case** from the header line, or create it from the template for its type.
2. **Save the body verbatim.** *prepare*: a new session file, with "Pasted in verbatim from another machine." under the title. *retro*: under Retro in that session's file (create the file if the game plan never arrived).
3. **File Case facts learned**, each in the one file where it holds: `case.md`, or higher when it holds unit- or client-wide.
4. **Check** Assumptions to confirm, and the body itself, against `client.md`, `structure.md` and `case.md`. List every conflict for Dave; unresolved ones go under Open questions in `case.md`.

**Done when** every item of Case facts learned and Assumptions to confirm is filed or listed as a conflict.

## Report

Writes `clients/<client>/report.md` from `templates/report.md`, over the previous one. Period: the previous calendar month, unless Dave names one. For Dave first; in English.

1. **Read** `client.md`, `structure.md`, every `case.md`, every session file dated in the period, the Evidence blocks of earlier sessions (for blockers still open), and `success-cases/`.
2. **Fill the template**, section by section:
   - **Seen in the work:** every Seen entry in the period with an artefact: a link, or an ID Dave can look up (PR !123, ticket ABC-456). A target behaviour counts once it is seen once with such an artefact; a Seen entry without one is reported as Said.
   - **Per case:** one block per team and individual case; where they stand in one line, ending in level range and client scale ("0–1, type 1"); target behaviours not yet seen; one Said; case blockers. What is in the Seen table is not repeated. Meeting and community cases show Said only. Cases without evidence (not started, paused, no retro yet) get one line with their route step and why.
   - **Blockers outside the teams:** every blocker not marked resolved in a later retro; *since* is the date of the first session whose Evidence names it.
   - **Success Case round:** only when a round falls in the period.
   - **Activity and context** last: session counts and context snapshots, labelled as context.
3. **Describe, never score.** Level and client scale appear as a description of where a case stands, never as movement or progress. Activity and Copilot numbers are context, never proof.

**Done when** every case of the client appears (a block or one line), every Seen entry in the period is in the table, and every open blocker is in the blockers table.

## Success Case round

Every six months per client, when Dave starts one (Report's In short reminds him when none was held yet or the last one is older than that).

1. **Pick** 1–2 cases with the strongest Seen evidence and 1 with little or none, from the case files and the last reports.
2. **Prepare the interviews** with Dave: per case, what to ask about what changed, what made the difference, what blocked it.
3. **After the interviews**, write `success-cases/YYYY-MM.md` from `templates/success-cases.md`: each story backed by evidence and naming its conditions. Take a context snapshot for the teams involved (a dated History line in each `case.md`).
4. **Climb:** blockers that hold beyond one case go to the unit in `structure.md` or to `client.md`.

**Done when** the round file holds at least one success and one non-success story, each with evidence and conditions.

## Bootstrap

Only when Dave asks for a new home repo, and only on his own machine (on a client machine, the paste block is the bridge):

1. Create an empty `clients/`.
2. Write `AGENTS.md` from `templates/AGENTS.md`; ask Dave how he opens the folders and fill in Paths and Skill source.
3. Write `CLAUDE.md` as the one line `@AGENTS.md`.
4. `git init`, no remote.

No client files until the first case.
