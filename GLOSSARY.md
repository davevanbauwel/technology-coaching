# Technology coaching

The words Dave and the `technology-coach` skill use for coaching software teams in AI-assisted delivery, and for the home repo where that coaching is recorded.

## Coaching record

**Home repo**:
Dave's private folder of coaching notes, one per machine he owns; never on client storage.
_Avoid_: coaching repo, dossier

**Client**:
An organisation where Dave coaches, holding facts that hold for every case there.
_Avoid_: customer, account

**Case**:
One coaching relationship Dave follows over time, with a goal, a level and a history.
_Avoid_: engagement, file, dossier

**Case type**:
The kind of case: team, individual, meeting or community.

**Team** (case type):
A group that works together and is coached over time.

**Individual** (case type):
One person coached one-on-one; always linked to the team they belong to.
_Avoid_: person case, coachee

**Meeting** (case type):
A one-off session for a mixed audience.
_Avoid_: event

**Community** (case type):
A recurring group with a changing audience, such as an AI champion forum or a knowledge-sharing series.
_Avoid_: forum (as a type), guild

**Unit**:
A part of the client's organisation (department, programme, domain), described in the structure file; not a folder level for cases.
_Avoid_: department (as a generic term)

**Structure file**:
The client's description of its units and of how Dave coaches in each: who sits where, his mandate, the route.
_Avoid_: org chart, unit file

**Route**:
The agreed sequence of sessions for the teams in a unit.
_Avoid_: programme, curriculum

**Route step**:
Where a case stands on its unit's route.
_Avoid_: journey stage

**Session**:
One meeting within a case, recorded as a game plan before and a retro after.
_Avoid_: appointment

**Paste block**:
The skill's output on a machine without a home repo (e.g. a client laptop): a game plan or retro plus the case facts learned and assumptions to confirm, pasted into the home repo later.
_Avoid_: export, handover

## Progress

**Level**:
The skill's 0–4 scale of where a team or person roughly stands in AI-assisted delivery; used to pitch a session, not to score progress. Ranges and grey areas are normal.
_Avoid_: rung, stage, maturity

**Client scale**:
A client's own adoption scale (e.g. type 1–3), mapped to levels in the client's file; like a level, a description, not a score.

**Report**:
One overview per client, generated from its case files and regenerated over the previous one; never maintained by hand.
_Avoid_: dashboard, status update

**Evidence**:
What shows a case is moving, weighed in three kinds: *seen* (behaviour in the work, with an artefact), *said* (what people report), *activity* (sessions held, attendance). Shows contribution, never causation.
_Avoid_: proof, KPI

**Success Case round**:
A periodic set of one or two success stories and one non-success story for a client, each backed by evidence and naming the conditions that made the difference.
_Avoid_: case study, testimonial

**Starting picture**:
Where a team or individual case began: goal, current way of working in words, target behaviours, context; filled in over the first sessions as it becomes clear.
_Avoid_: baseline, intake assessment

**Target behaviour**:
An observable practice a team or person agrees to grow into, such as starting a story from an agent with the repo's context file; progress is these showing up in the work, seen once with convincing proof.
_Avoid_: KPI, goal, level-up
